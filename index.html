§ 01 — The Pipeline
A model is grown through a process, not written by hand.

How a model is grown, stage by stage.

01
Data Prep

Data Collection

Gather raw text at planetary scale — web crawls, books, code repositories, articles, and forums. Frontier models ingest 10–15+ trillion tokens.

02
Data Prep

Preprocessing & Tokenization

Clean, deduplicate, and filter out low-quality or toxic content, then split text into subword "tokens." Data quality now rivals quantity in importance.

03
Compute Core

Pre-training

The compute-heavy core. The model learns language by predicting the next token across the entire corpus, tuning billions–trillions of parameters over weeks to months. Most of the money, energy, and water are spent here.

04
Align & Tune

Supervised Fine-Tuning

Train the base model on curated instruction–response pairs so it stops merely predicting text and starts behaving like a helpful assistant.

05
Align & Tune

Alignment — RLHF & Constitutional AI

Use human or AI preference feedback to steer outputs toward being helpful, honest, and harmless. This stage shapes the model's behavior and safety.

06
Ship & Serve

Evaluation & Red-Teaming

Benchmark capabilities and stress-test for safety, bias, and failure modes. Failures here send the model back for more tuning.

07
Ship & Serve

Deployment & Inference

Serve the model through APIs and apps, then monitor and iterate. Training is a one-time cost — inference runs forever and can dominate lifetime energy use.

§ 02 — The Resource Bill
Six categories that scale together as models grow.

What a single training run consumes.

Datasets
15T+ tokens

GPT-4 trained on ~13T tokens; Llama 3.1 on 15T+. Cleaning and human-annotating that data is now among the largest costs — sometimes exceeding compute.

Compute
25,000 A100 GPUs

GPT-4 reportedly used ~25,000 A100s for ~90–100 days. Llama 3.1 405B used 16,000 H100s — roughly 30.8 million GPU-hours.

Energy
21.6 GWh / run

One Llama 3.1 405B run drew an estimated 21.6 GWh — about 17× GPT-3's 1,287 MWh. Energy use climbs even as chips get more efficient.

Water
700,000 liters

Training GPT-3 evaporated ~700,000 L of freshwater for cooling (~5.4M L including electricity) — enough to build roughly 370 cars.

Time
~80 days

A single frontier pre-training run spans weeks to months. The full development cycle — experiments, tuning, evaluation — runs far longer.

Cost
$78M+ compute

GPT-4's compute alone is estimated at $63–78M, with all-in costs over $100M. Google's Gemini Ultra is estimated near $192M.

§ 04 — Why It Matters
Each resource shapes who can build, and at what price.

Why the resource bill shapes everything.

Compute is a barrier to entry.

Clusters of tens of thousands of GPUs cost hundreds of millions. Only a handful of organizations can train at the frontier — concentrating power in few hands.

Energy & water are real externalities.

Training and serving draw on the same grids and watersheds as communities. Efficiency gains are repeatedly outpaced by growth in model size.

Data quality drives performance.

More tokens help, but clean, diverse, well-annotated data increasingly decides whether a model is good — and annotation labor is now a top cost.

Inference is the long tail.

Training is a one-time spend; inference runs on every prompt and can dominate a model's lifetime energy footprint.
